How the Booking Process Works

Step 1: Search or Request Assistance

Customers can explore nationwide bus routes and schedules directly on our website or contact our dedicated bus specialists by phone if they need help comparing standard bus, express coach, and intercity coach options.

Step 2: Explore and Compare Options

Travelers can compare popular interstate and regional express bus departures across standard coach and luxury express classes based on travel dates, schedule, and budget.

Step 3: Select Travel Itinerary

Once your preferred bus route, departure terminal, travel time, and seat options are chosen, lock in that itinerary for booking.

Step 4: Review Pricing Details

Before proceeding to payment, customers are shown the complete package total upfront. This may include:

  • Base bus fare & onboard amenities
  • Standard or premium seating reservations
  • Applicable taxes & assisted booking fees

All pricing details are presented before final confirmation.

Step 5: Payment Processing

Payment is securely processed by trainhello.online using authorized payment systems. Customers are not redirected to unrelated third-party payment entities.

Step 6: Booking Submission and Processing

After payment is completed, your bus reservation request is submitted for real-time seat allocation and digital boarding pass generation.

Step 7: Confirmation of Booking

Once the booking is successfully processed, confirmation details are generated. Customers receive:

  • Bus reservation confirmation code (PNR)
  • Bus schedule, coach seat & terminal details
  • Passenger information

Step 8: Ticket Delivery Timeline

In most cases, bus confirmation documents and mobile QR passes are delivered promptly after processing, along with terminal check-in, boarding gate, and luggage instructions for your departure station or bus terminal.

Step 9: Verification with Bus Carrier

Customers can verify their bus reservation directly with the operating bus carrier using the confirmation number (PNR) provided before departure.

Step 10: Post-Booking Assistance (Optional)

Customers may contact our support team for assistance with:

  • Departure date or class upgrades
  • Cancellations
  • Seat selection & baggage queries

All changes and cancellations are subject to bus carrier policies and fare rules.
