Baggage Policies & Terminal Check-In Guidelines

Carry-On Luggage

One small bag or backpack (under 25 lbs) is permitted inside the passenger cabin. Must fit in the overhead storage bin or underneath the seat in front of you.

Checked Undercarriage Bags

Most routes include 1 to 2 checked bags (up to 50 lbs / 23 kg each). Checked bags are tagged and safely loaded into the bus lower luggage bays by the driver.

Boarding With Phone E-Ticket

No printer required! Show your scannable digital boarding pass barcode and a valid government photo ID to the driver upon boarding at the terminal bay.
